🧬 TWO ARCHITECTURES

Carbon intelligence vs Silicon intelligence.

The class compared the biological and artificial foundations of intelligence.

🧠 Natural Intelligence

BASE ELEMENT Carbon
STRUCTURE Neurons + Synapses
ENERGY Chemical processes / food
BIOLOGICAL LIMIT Sleep, fatigue and biological lifespan

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

BASE ELEMENT Silicon
STRUCTURE Artificial Neural Networks
ENERGY Electrical power
COMPUTATIONAL ADVANTAGE Continuous large-scale computation
⚡ THE SILICON MIND

How did we reach modern AI?

Day 1 travelled through major milestones before arriving at Generative AI.

1950

Alan Turing

The class explored the question of whether machine behaviour could become indistinguishable from human behaviour.

1956

Dartmouth & John McCarthy

The classroom journey connected this period with the formal emergence of the term Artificial Intelligence.

1997

Deep Blue

A landmark example of machine computation: IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov.

2016

AlphaGo

Another major milestone arrived when AlphaGo defeated Lee Sedol in the complex game of Go.

2017

The Transformer Era

Transformer architecture marked a major shift toward machines working more effectively with natural human language and context.

🤖 DECODING MODERN AI

What does GPT actually mean?

Three letters that helped us understand the architecture behind modern Generative AI.

G

Generative

The output engine — creating new text, images and solutions.

P

Pre-trained

The knowledge base — trained extensively on large collections of human-created data before interaction.

T

Transformer

The comprehension architecture — working with context and relationships within language.

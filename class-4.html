🔍 OBSERVE LIKE A BUILDER

Don't just open the websites. Study them.

Every student project can teach you something. Look beyond the final appearance and notice how the website has been structured.

🧭

Navigation

How does the website help the visitor move from one section to another?

🎨

Visual Identity

Notice colours, spacing, typography and the overall visual personality.

🧱

Structure

What sections appear first? How is information grouped and presented?

📱

Mobile Experience

Open the site on your phone. Does everything remain readable, usable and properly aligned?

⚙️

Functionality

Test buttons, links, menus and interactive elements.

💡

The Idea

Most importantly: what real need or idea is this website trying to serve?

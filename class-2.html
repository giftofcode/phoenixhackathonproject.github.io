🎯 THE CORE FRAMEWORK

C → T → F

One simple structure turns a vague request into a much more deliberate AI instruction.

C

Context

Give AI the background it needs. Who are you? Who is the audience? What is the situation? Why is this output needed?

T

Task

State exactly what AI should do. Explain. Compare. Create. Analyse. Rewrite. Design. Summarise.

F

Format

Define how you want the result delivered. A table? Five bullets? A 30-second script? A professional email? Step-by-step instructions?

💡 TRY TO REMEMBER

🧩 CHUNKING

Big task? Don't throw everything at AI at once.

Complex work becomes easier to control when it is divided into smaller, logical stages.

01

Define the objective

Decide what the final outcome actually needs to accomplish.

02

Break the task down

Divide one large request into smaller manageable components.

03

Work stage by stage

Complete and review one part before moving to the next.

04

Combine the pieces

Bring the verified outputs together into the final result.

⚠️ THE CRITICAL WARNING

AI can sound confident and still be wrong.

Day 2 introduced one of the most important ideas for responsible AI use: hallucination.

AI HALLUCINATION

Fluent ≠ Factual

An AI system may produce information that sounds convincing even when the information is inaccurate, unsupported or fabricated. That is why human judgment remains part of the workflow.

🔍 Verify Check important facts against reliable sources.
🧠 Question Do not accept an answer simply because it sounds confident.
👤 Human Oversight The final responsibility remains with the human user.
🤝 THE REAL WORKFLOW

AI generates. Humans judge.

The strongest workflow is not “AI instead of humans.” It is structured human thinking working together with machine capability.

MACHINE ROLE

Generate & Accelerate

Draft, explain, organise, transform and generate possibilities at speed.

+
HUMAN ROLE

Decide & Verify

Apply judgment, context, ethics, factual verification and final responsibility.
